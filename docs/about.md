Hello, I'm <span class="wiggle mono">Rhys</span>.

I live in Kent with my wonderful wife [Katie - a Kent-based wedding photographer](https://www.katiemillsphotography.co.uk/) – our sons Elliott and Timothy, and our cat Walter. When I find some spare time (not very often) I like hiking, bouldering and live music.

Professionally, I'm a Senior Software Engineer at [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/). Mostly this website is written from that perspective. I work on full stack projects incorporating frontend (usually Typescript/JavaScript & React), backend (usually Scala) and infrastructure work (mostly in AWS).

I've worked on a few teams at the Guardian, currently I work in the Ophan team, developing The Guardian's in-house pageview analytics platform. Recently I've worked on projects like:

- Leading development on a data pipeline, pulling suspected bot pageviews from BigQuery and amending pageview documents in our Elasticsearch cluster
- Pulling social media data into Ophan and adding new dashboards to view it alongside our pageview data
- Enhancing pages that give staff access to long term historical pageview data

Before that, I spent a few years in the Editorial Tools team, l worked on projects like:
- Leading development on new a system across our CMS and content API to support new rich-media structured article formats like Timelines
- [Creating a new spellchecker](https://theguardian.engineering/blog/info-2023-dec-11-making-typerighter-work-harder-engineering-blog.html) for The Guardian's ProseMirror-based CMS
- Migrating text fields across the Tools estate to ProseMirror
